Appendix A

Sources and Next Steps

How this handbook was made, free places to keep learning, and what to do next.

#A.1 How this handbook was made

  • All explanations, code, exercises, projects and synthetic datasets were written for this handbook by MeritaAnalytics.
  • The choice of topics follows the standard path of an introductory data science curriculum.
  • Real data comes from open sources credited in Appendix B. Open-source libraries do the computing: NumPy, pandas, matplotlib, seaborn, SciPy, statsmodels, scikit-learn, XGBoost, LightGBM and PyTorch.
